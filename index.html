About Indo Canadian Carriers

Leaders in refrigerated transportation with over a decade of industry experience

Our Story

Indo Canadian Carriers began operations in February 2014 under the leadership of Mr. Vipen Chauser (President), Rupinder Kaur (Director), and Jaswinder Soni (Operations Manager). With over 10 years of hands-on experience in trucking transportation, our founders brought immense industry expertise to establish a company focused on quality service.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, affordable, and the best transportation service to various industries through quality service, cutting-edge technology, and professional expertise.

We operate with dedicated office and yard space, complete with on-site fleet maintenance to keep our vehicles in top condition. Quality service is the core of our company and sets us apart from other transportation providers.

Our Transportation Services

Specialized temperature-controlled transportation solutions for various industries

FTL Shipments

We transport Full Truckload (FTL) shipments throughout Canada and the United States to meet your supply chain needs.

  • Dedicated trucks for your shipment
  • Faster transit times
  • Reduced handling of goods

Temperature-Controlled Transport

Our temperature controlled trailers can be adjusted to maintain an internal trailer environment within a specific temperature range.

  • Precise temperature control
